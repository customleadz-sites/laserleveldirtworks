Common Questions

Land Clearing Questions

How much does land clearing cost?

It depends on the acreage, how thick the brush is, and how much debris has to be hauled off. Estimates are free — the owner walks the property with you and gives you a written quote before any work starts.

What do you clear?

Brush, undergrowth, thickets, small trees, and debris — cleared clean and hauled off, no burn piles left behind. Large tree removal and stump grinding aren't services we offer.

What's the land like when you're done?

Cleared, hauled off, and finish-graded with laser-guided equipment so it drains right and is ready to build on or use.

How long does it take?

That depends on the acreage and what's on it. You'll get a timeline along with your written quote at the estimate.

What happens after I call or submit the form?

You'll talk directly with the owner. We set up a free on-site estimate, you get a written quote, and you decide — no obligation, no pressure.
